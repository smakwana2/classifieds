1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your username, password and, if you choose to give it, your email address. We store your password only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read. We also record when you joined and when you accepted our Terms of Use.
  • Ads: the title, description, price, category, tags, location name and photos you post, and the map pin if you add one.
  • Messages: the messages you send to other users, when they were sent, and whether they have been read.

Information collected automatically

  • Location, only when you ask for it. If you click “Use my location” or the locate button in the search bar, your browser asks your permission and then shares your position with the page. We round it to about 100 meters before using it. When you post an ad, the rounded pin is saved with the ad; when you search, it is used for that search and not saved to your account. You can always type a place name instead.
  • Photos are cleaned. Photos taken on phones usually contain hidden data, including the GPS position where they were taken and the device model. We remove all of it, and the original file name, before any photo is stored or shown.
  • Technical information: your IP address and basic request details (pages requested, time, browser type), as every website receives. We use your IP address to limit abuse, such as repeated failed logins, and our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

2. How we use it

  • To run the site: show your ads, let buyers and sellers message each other, keep you logged in, and find ads near a place you search for.
  • To keep the site safe: prevent spam, fraud and password guessing, enforce our Terms of Use, and comply with the law.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't build profiles about you, and we don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. What other people can see

  • Public: your username, the month you joined, and everything in your ads (including photos, the location name and the approximate map pin). Anyone on the internet can see these.
  • Only the other person in a conversation: your messages. We may review messages only to investigate a report of abuse or fraud, or when the law requires it.
  • Never shown: your email address and your password.

Think before you post: don't put your home address, phone number or other personal details in an ad.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as those terms are defined in California law). We have not done so in the past 12 months. We share information only in these cases:

  • Map tiles (OpenStreetMap): when a page shows a map, your browser loads map images directly from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers, which therefore receive your IP address and the map area being viewed. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Place search (OpenStreetMap Nominatim): when you search by a place name, our server sends the text you typed (for example “Oakland, CA”) to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find its coordinates. Your IP address and account are not sent with it.
  • Service providers: companies that host and run the site for us, who may process data only on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, a court order or other legal process, or when needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us.
  • A change of ownership: if the site is sold or transferred, your information would move to the new operator, who must follow this policy or tell you about changes.

California's “Shine the Light” law lets California residents ask which personal information we shared with third parties for their own direct marketing. We don't share personal information for that purpose.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information: until you delete your account.
  • Ads and their photos: until you delete the ad or your account.
  • Messages: until the ad is deleted or either person in the conversation deletes their account.
  • Abuse-prevention records (counts linked to an IP address or account): up to 1 day.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Backups: deleted information may stay in backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.

We may keep information longer when the law requires it or to resolve a dispute or investigate abuse.

7. Your choices

  • Edit or delete any of your ads from My ads.
  • Delete your account at any time from My ads › Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, ads, photos and conversations.
  • Location is optional: you can post and search without ever sharing your position.
  • Email us to get a copy of your information or to correct it.

8. Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) gives you these rights, and we extend them to all users:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and to get a copy of it.
  • Right to delete your personal information (you can also do this yourself by deleting your account).
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We don't sell or share it, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. The only sensitive information we handle is your password and, if you choose to provide it, an approximate location; we use them only to provide the service you asked for.
  • Right not to be discriminated against for using any of these rights.

9. Children

Classifieds is not for children. You must be at least {{ minimum_age }} to create an account, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

We protect your information with HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords, limits on login attempts, and access controls on private data such as messages. No website can be perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else. If a security breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as required by law.
